## Problems found and fixed during the run

- Lane C's two wait loops ran `pgrep -f`, which matched their own command line, so they never ended (stuck 6 h). Wait loops must not search for their own command line.
- Step 5's "Part 1 of 4" label pushed the phone slider below the fold (644 px against the 620 px limit). Four phone gaps were tightened; it is now at 614 px.
- A negative margin cannot remove the grid gap above an empty line. Empty message lines are now taken out of the flow with `position: absolute`.
- The relativity rebuild replaced the no-break space before "m" in `formatMetres` with a plain one. Restored.
- Element screenshots caught sticky cards mid-section. The section crops are now taken from a full-page capture at scroll 0.
