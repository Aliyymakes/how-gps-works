## Final re-audit (item 12, Fable, on the live site)

Of the 26 morning findings: 19 closed, 5 partly, 0 open, 2 explained. The re-audit also found 8 new problems (N1-N8). Seven were fixed in #74 before the freeze, with 13 new tests × 2 browsers:
- N2, a real bug: the hints in sections 2 to 4 were invisible (opacity 0). The item 4 test had missed it because Playwright's is_visible ignores opacity.
- N3: "Back to trusting the clock…" was erased during a drag.
- N1: the closing line now scrolls into view.
- N6: the guide copes with steps done out of order.
- N5: the loading words no longer cross the map mark.
- N7: Try again now answers while still offline.
- N8: no half sentence while loading.

#74 also fixed C6: a missing file of ours is no longer blamed on the internet. N4 (phones) is left for Icarus: question 1.

Partly closed, and why:
- **B1, slow first load.** The page now says what it is waiting for in every section, but the download is about 9 MB from the Pyodide CDN. First visits took 3.8 to 30.9 s in Chromium (median 7.5 s) and 5.9 to 101 s in WebKit (median 13.2 s); 7 of 24 visits took over 30 s. Warm visits take 1.5 to 5.4 s. Cold timing depends on the CDN and the network, not on our code.
- **B6** and **C6**: fixed in #74 (N3 and the missing-file words); listed as partly only because the re-audit ran before #74.
- **C13, jargon.** "Fix", "DOP" and "µs" are explained where they first appear, but the page still uses "fix" often.
- **P4, empty message lines.** The lines above controls keep their space on purpose, so nothing jumps while dragging.

Explained, not bugs:
- **C12.** A freeze of about 1.0 to 1.4 s while Python starts; only a Web Worker could split it.
- **C14.** In Safari, plain Tab skips controls by default (Option+Tab reaches them).

Two-minute verdict (Fable): yes on a normal load on a laptop. At risk when the first download is slow, and on phones (see question 1).
