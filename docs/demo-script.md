# Demo video script

**Draft for Icarus.** Shot list for the short screen recording the brief asks us to submit (design.md section 1). Order follows the depth ladder (2a): the first screen's two-minute idea, then rung 2, then rung 3. Every voice-over line is a draft. No number is quoted from outside the page: the voice-over points at the readouts instead. Control names for rungs 2 and 3 are placeholders until those screens exist; the first-screen names come from design.md 2b. Total length and per-shot timing are not set (see Questions).

| # | Shot | What is on screen | What the user does | Voice-over line (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Load | The first screen: ground map on the left, 4 satellites, distance lines meeting at the solved dot, green fix | Nothing. Let it sit | "Your phone finds where it is by timing signals from satellites." |
| 2 | Wrong clock | Right panel: "Make the phone's clock wrong" slider highlighted | Drags the slider away from zero | "But the phone's clock is cheap, and a small time error becomes a long distance error." |
| 3 | Lines pull apart | Distance lines spread; dot moves; readout "Position off by N m" grows | Keeps dragging, then holds | "The lines no longer meet. The readout shows how far off the position is." |
| 4 | Solve the clock | Button "Let the phone solve for its clock" | Presses it | "Now the phone treats its own clock as unknown and solves for it too." |
| 5 | Lines meet | Lines redraw and meet; page states how fast or slow the clock was | Holds on the sentence | "The lines meet again, and the page says how wrong the clock was." |
| 6 | Take one away | Per-satellite switches | Switches one satellite off | "Take one satellite away..." |
| 7 | Refusal | Page refuses in one sentence: four unknowns (east, north, up, clock), three measurements | Holds on the sentence | "...and it refuses: four unknowns, only three measurements." |
| 8 | Bunch them (rung 2) | Sky view with satellites; DOP and position error readouts (placeholder control: satellite spread) | Drags the satellites closer together | "Same error, but with the satellites bunched together the fix gets much worse." |
| 9 | Read the geometry | DOP readout and position error readout both larger | Holds, then spreads them out again | "DOP says how much the layout multiplies the error. Spread out, it shrinks." |
| 10 | Clean signal (rung 3) | Correlation plot with a clear peak (placeholder control: noise) | Presses play on the sliding code, or moves the delay | "The phone finds the signal by sliding a known code along it until it lines up." |
| 11 | Add noise | Same plot, noise slider (placeholder name) | Raises noise slowly | "Add noise and the peak holds, even though the signal is weaker than the noise." |
| 12 | Peak lost | Peak sinks into the noise; best delay jumps to a wrong one | Raises noise further | "Past a point the peak is lost, and the phone no longer knows the time." |

## Questions for Icarus

1. **Total length and time per shot.** The brief asks for a "short" video; the spec gives no length. Recommendation: you pick the total, and the recorder times shots after a dry run. Shots 1 to 7 are the part every judge must understand, so give them most of the time and cut from the end (shots 8 to 12) if the limit is tight.
2. **Who records and narrates.** The spec leaves this TBA. Recommendation: one teammate records and talks, so the voice is steady and that person can explain the page in the interview. Your call.
3. **Voice-over or on-screen captions.** Recommendation: spoken voice-over, with the page's own sentences visible on screen.
4. **Rungs 2 and 3 control names.** Placeholders above. Recommendation: update shots 8 to 12 once those screens are merged, before recording.
